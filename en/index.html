Career

My career brings together research in computing, advising students and leading initiatives that connect science, education and the application of artificial intelligence.

My path in computing

My path in computing began in 1995, in a technical course in data processing. Later, I chose an academic career. I hold a BSc in Computer Science from Unesp and a master's and PhD from USP, and I have been a professor at UFG since 2009.

My work combines research, advising and academic management. In my master's, I investigated algorithms for predicting protein structures. In my PhD, I worked on data structures for algorithms applied to network problems. Today, my research topics include reinforcement learning and optimization.

I have also worked in the management of the graduate programme in Computing and in creating new educational opportunities. I took part in building UFG's Bachelor's degree in Artificial Intelligence and in founding CEIA in 2019. Since 2022, I have been the centre's executive director.

Problems I investigate

Conference paper · ICML · 2025

Sliding Puzzles Gym

An environment for evaluating visual representation in reinforcement learning

Co-authored work proposing a puzzle-based test environment. It makes it possible to study how AI agents extract information from images to make decisions.

Bryan Lincoln Marques de Oliveira, Luana Guedes Barros Martins, Bruno Brandão, Murilo Lopes da Luz, Telma Woerle de Lima Soares and Luckeciano Carvalho Melo.

Read the paper in the ICML proceedings (opens in a new tab)
Doctoral thesis · USP · 2009

Estruturas de dados eficientes para algoritmos evolutivos aplicados a projeto de redes

Efficient data structures for evolutionary algorithms applied to network design

Research on ways of representing networks to make optimization algorithms more efficient. The thesis includes an application to the reconfiguration of power distribution systems.

Author: Telma Woerle de Lima Soares. Advisor: Alexandre Cláudio Botazzo Delbem.

See the thesis (opens in a new tab)
Master's dissertation · USP · 2006

Algoritmos evolutivos para predição de estruturas de proteínas

Evolutionary algorithms for protein structure prediction

An investigation of computational methods for estimating the three-dimensional structure of proteins from their sequence. The work uses evolutionary algorithms, which explore alternatives through successive modification and selection of solutions.

Author: Telma Woerle de Lima. Advisor: Alexandre Cláudio Botazzo Delbem.

    Short bio

    Telma Soares is a professor and researcher at the Institute of Informatics of the Federal University of Goiás (UFG), Brazil. She is co-founder of CEIA, the Center of Excellence in Artificial Intelligence, and has been its executive director since 2022.

    Full bio

    Telma Soares has been a professor and researcher at the Institute of Informatics of the Federal University of Goiás (UFG), Brazil, since 2009. She holds a BSc in Computer Science from Unesp and a master's and PhD from the University of São Paulo (USP). Her research covers evolutionary algorithms, reinforcement learning and optimization. She worked in the management of the graduate programme in Computer Science and took part in building UFG's Bachelor's degree in Artificial Intelligence. She is co-founder of the Center of Excellence in Artificial Intelligence (CEIA), created in 2019, and has been its executive director since 2022. In 2025 she placed 2nd in the Outstanding Researcher category of the Embrapii Award.
